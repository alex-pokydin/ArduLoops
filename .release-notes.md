### What's in 2.4.5

You can move the assistant, live charts and documents to their own windows, for example on a second monitor. The Frame step for planes shows the airframe and its outputs.

- **Separate windows**  - The assistant, Live lines, finding charts, documents and the board window have a new button in the header. Click it to open the panel in its own window. Move that window to a different monitor.
- **Window places**  - The app keeps the size and position of each window. When you open a window again, it opens at the same place.
- **Pause and time span**  - Pause and the plot time span are the same in all windows.
- **Assistant window**  - Ask and Fix buttons send the question to the assistant window. The assistant window knows which Setup step you look at. Options open in the main window.
- **Close**  - When you close the main window, the other windows close too.
- **Plane airframe**  - The Frame step shows the airframe of your plane. Each surface and motor shows its output. Change surfaces, QuadPlane lift and outputs in the Airframe wizard.
- **Plane safety**  - The Airframe wizard writes only to a plane. Before, it could write outputs to a copter when the plane view was open.
