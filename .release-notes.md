### What's in 2.4.3

Setup wizards now apply their result. When ArduPilot reads a setting only at boot, one button saves it and reboots the board.

- **Board orientation**  - The detected orientation is saved with Save & reboot. This also works when the assistant opens the wizard. Set level again after the reboot.
- **Compass orientation**  - Exact angles are saved with Save & reboot. The heading check starts when the board is back.
- **Battery sensor and frame**  - The last step shows the change and saves it with Save & reboot. Nothing is written before that step.
- **Step pages**  - When a change needs a reboot, Write becomes Save & reboot. You do not have to find the reboot button in the header.
