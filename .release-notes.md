### What's in 2.4.0

The Controller Setup tab is new. Each setup step shows the vehicle live, lists its jobs in order, and opens a guided window for each calibration.

- **Vehicle header**  - The header shows the vehicle name, note, profile, and live health tiles for pre-arm, GPS, EKF, battery, and RC.
- **Setup overview**  - The Overview shows which steps block arming. It shows the pre-arm lines and the skipped checks. A window sets which arming checks stop arming.
- **Frame and outputs**  - The Frame step picks the frame from drawings. The motor wizard finds the motor order and direction, and spins each motor at idle.
- **Accelerometer**  - The step shows a live horizon. Guided windows do the six-side calibration and find the board orientation.
- **Compass**  - The step sets compass priority, ignored types, and turning the compass off. Guided windows do the calibration with a rotating coverage sphere, the mount angle, and motor interference.
- **Battery**  - The step shows the pack against its warning levels. Guided windows match the voltage to a meter and the current to a charger.
- **Radio**  - The step shows a transmitter with the live sticks. Guided windows calibrate the stick ends and check each stick direction.
- **Flight modes and failsafes**  - The flight modes step finds the mode switch and shows which modes need GPS or flow. The failsafes step shows each trigger live and tests the radio failsafe.
- **Vehicle profile**  - A short form stores props, cells, weight, and flying style. The assistant uses it for starting values.
- **Assistant**  - The assistant opens every setup window as a card and gets the result back. Fix on a pre-arm line sends a request that matches the cause.
- **Virtual sticks**  - The app sends RC overrides only while the virtual sticks are on. It releases them after 10 seconds when the vehicle is disarmed.
- **Faster parameter load**  - The app loads all parameters as one file over MAVFTP when the board supports it.
