### What's in 2.4.4

The Setup Overview now shows the documentation for your flight controller. The header loads the vehicle name and note every time.

- **Board doc**  - The Overview shows the README of your board from the ArduPilot source. The app saves it, so it also works offline.
- **Board window**  - Open the README, hwdef.dat and defaults.parm in a window. Move the window and change its size. It stays open when you go to other tabs.
- **hwdef.dat**  - The file shows with line numbers, colors and search. Click an include line to open that file.
- **Images**  - Click a board image to see it full screen. Use the mouse wheel to zoom and drag to move it.
- **Overview**  - The progress ring is on the left. The pre-arm checks and the next steps are next to it.
- **Header**  - The name and note have more space. Back up and Reboot are in the ⋯ menu.
- **Vehicle name and note**  - Sometimes the header did not show them after you connected. This is fixed.
- **Vehicle profile**  - The header form has Cancel. When Save is off, the form tells you why.
