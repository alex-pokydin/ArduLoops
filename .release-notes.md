### What's in 2.2.4

SITL can be opened from Mission Planner, and a mission written here can be read back there.

- **SITL links** — The simulator also listens on TCP 5760 and sends UDP to 127.0.0.1:14550. While it is running, the panel lists those addresses.
- **Mission** — Waypoints go out as MISSION_ITEM_INT. Home is an absolute altitude, so Mission Planner can download the plan.
- **SITL panel** — Init is its own section and only writes a linked, disarmed SITL. Headers have icons. A fault that is on, GPS turned off, or wind that is not zero shows on the closed header. Speedup shows the current rate.
- **Own key** — A rejected key, an empty quota, or a missing model stays on the form with a short reason. The key you typed is kept.
- **Links** — An https link opens in the system browser.
