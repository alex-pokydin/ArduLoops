### What's in 2.2.3

Connect keeps ATTITUDE flowing before it loads the rest of the vehicle. The side log stays on one line, and you can change its width.

- **Connect**  - The link asks for ATTITUDE first. Other streams and parameters load after that, in small batches. This keeps SITL and the board connected.
- **Status**  - The header shows the frame, the source, and the rate in Hz. A thin bar under that text shows parameter load.
- **Log**  - Each line shows minutes and seconds. Text that does not fit is hidden. Drag the short mark between the panes to change the side panel width.
